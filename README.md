**Audio Traveler**
##### A SwiftUI-based iOS app that allows users to record audio logs tied to specific geo-locations and play them back later. Perfect for travelers who want to capture and revisit their memories based on the places they've visited.

**Features**
---------------------
**Login & Registration:**

* Simple and secure user authentication with the ability to sign up or log in.
* Login and account creation screens have clear and intuitive UI with password visibility toggles.

**Home Screen:** 
* A dashboard that shows recent audio records and navigational options at the bottom.
* Displays time-stamped records with geo-location, allowing users to see where they recorded their audio.

** Notifications:** 
* Alerts for important updates, such as newly added audio logs or other actions related to your journey.

**Geo-Location-Based Audio Logs:**
* Record audio logs based on your current location, and automatically tie the recording to a specific place.
* View these audio logs on a map, with each record displayed as a pin, making it easy to navigate and revisit recordings based on their location.

**Map Integration:**
* Navigate the map to see all your audio logs in one place. Tap on any pin to listen to the audio recorded at that location.
* Playback controls are built into the map interface for a seamless experience.

**Audio Playback Controls:**
* Intuitive audio player with simple controls like rewind, play/pause, and fast forward.
* Each playback session is tied to the location where the audio was originally recorded, allowing users to "travel" through their memories.

**Dependencies**
--------------------
##### MapKit: For map functionalities, such as placing pins and navigating between locations.
##### AVFoundation: For handling audio recording and playback.
##### CoreLocation: To tie audio logs to specific geo-locations.
